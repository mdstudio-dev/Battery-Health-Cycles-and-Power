Privacy Policy for Battery Health, Cycles and Power
Effective Date: October 15, 2026 Last Updated: October 15, 2026
Battery Health, Cycles and Power ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application (the "App").
1. Local Data Storage & Collection
A. Battery Metrics & Logs
The App reads hardware battery state from your device using standard Android APIs (such as BatteryManager). This includes:
•
Battery percentage level (%)
•
Battery voltage (mV) and temperature (°C)
•
Charging current (mA)
•
Estimated capacity (mAh) and charge cycle counts
•
Charging session start/end timestamps and peak temperatures
Important: All battery metrics, session logs, and history are stored 100% locally on your device in an encrypted on-device database (Room). We do not collect, transmit, upload, or store your battery metrics on any external server or cloud service.
2. Third-Party Advertising & Consent
A. Google AdMob & UMP SDK
The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to manage user consent.
AdMob may collect and process certain device information to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements, including:
•
Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID)
•
IP address and general geographic location (country/city level)
•
Device hardware specifications and performance data
For full details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.
B. GDPR & European Economic Area (EEA) / UK Rights
If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom (UK):
•
We request your explicit consent before loading personalized ads via the Google UMP consent form.
•
You can change or revoke your privacy consent choices at any time inside the App by navigating to Settings > Privacy & ad choices (GDPR).
3. Data Retention & User Deletion Control
You have total control over your local battery data:
•
You can clear all local battery history, samples, and session records instantly by opening the App and tapping Settings > Delete all stored battery data.
4. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly within the App or on our official store listing page.
